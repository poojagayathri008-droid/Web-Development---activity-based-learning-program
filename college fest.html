A Few Fest Rules 💛

It's all fun and games, but let's keep it awesome for everyone!

  1. Bring your college ID and register for the events you want to join.
  2. Reach the venue on time. The best moments shouldn't happen without you!
  3. Cheer for everyone. We're here to celebrate talent, not put people down.
  4. Keep the campus clean. Use bins and respect the fest spaces.
  5. Ask permission before posting someone else's close-up photos or videos.
  6. Follow stage, crowd-safety and event-coordinator instructions.
  7. Respect everyone's choices, personal space and performances.
  8. Most importantly, have fun and make memories responsibly!
